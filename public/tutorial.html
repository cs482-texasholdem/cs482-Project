Section 1 · High-Level Overview

Welcome to No-Limit Texas Hold'em

No prior poker experience required—here is how a hand works from start to finish.

The Goal

Win the Total Pot (the pile of chips bet during the hand). You win either by making the best 5-card poker hand at the end, or by making every other player fold before the end.

Your Cards & Community Cards

Every player gets 2 private hole cards face down. Up to 5 shared community cards are dealt face up in the middle of the table. You combine any 5 of those 7 cards to make your best hand.

The 4 Betting Rounds
  • Pre-Flop: Everyone receives 2 private cards.
  • Flop: 3 community cards are revealed.
  • Turn: A 4th community card is revealed.
  • River & Showdown: A 5th card is revealed, followed by final betting and revealing hands.

Tie-Breaking Rule

If two players hold the exact same hand category, higher card ranks break the tie. If all 5 best cards match in rank, the pot is split equally!

Section 4 · Forced Bets (Blinds)

How Small & Big Blinds Kick Off Every Hand

Now that you know how betting works, here is why there is already money in the pot before cards are dealt.

Dealer Button (D)
Rotates Clockwise

The D badge marks the nominal dealer. It moves one seat clockwise after every hand so everyone takes turns paying blinds fairly.

Small Blind (SB)
Half Forced Bet

The seat left of the Dealer posts the Small Blind automatically (e.g., $10 in a $10/$20 game). In a 2-player heads-up hand, the Dealer posts the Small Blind.

Big Blind (BB)
Full Forced Bet

The next seat posts the Big Blind (e.g., $20), which sets the initial Current Highest Bet that Pre-Flop players must at least Call to stay in.

Section 5 · App Showdown & Privacy

How Mucking Works in Our App

Control whether opponents get to see your losing cards at Showdown.

  • 1. Folding Automatically Mucks: Whenever you click Fold, your cards are immediately discarded face-down so nobody sees them.
  • 2. "Muck Losing Cards" Switch: In our betting panel, toggling Muck Losing Cards ON hides your hole cards at Showdown if another player beats your hand.
  • 3. Winners Always Reveal: If you win the pot at Showdown, your winning hand is always shown to prove the win—even if Muck Losing Cards is switched on.
